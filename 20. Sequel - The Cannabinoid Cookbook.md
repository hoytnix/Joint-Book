### An Introduction to the Sequel: *The Cannabinoid Cookbook*

For years, the standard street playbook has treated synthetic drugs, heavy sedatives, and adulterated street pharmaceuticals as unavoidable hazards. But having spent years inside large-scale commercial extraction facilities—first scaling processing operations in Texas, then running high-throughput production labs in Michigan—the reality became undeniable: **cannabinoids can replace almost damn near all of them**.

The next volume tackles the other half of the equation.

If *What Was In That Joint Bro!?* provides the defensive radar to detect adulterated garbage and stay alive, the upcoming cookbook lays out the proactive substitution framework: how natural phytocannabinoid formulations, minor cannabinoid ratios, and targeted plant preparations can systematically displace the toxic, unpredictable pharmaceuticals dominating the street.

The testing protocol in your hands is its lifelong companion. Verify what's real, clear out the poison, and build the clean alternative.
