## Glossary of Plain-English Terms

* <span id="term-adulterant"><a href="#term-adulterant" class="glossary-anchor">**Adulterant:**</a></span> A foreign, unlisted chemical agent, synthetic drug, or bulking oil added to a substance to mimic potency, cut production costs, or stretch supply weight.
* <span id="term-capillary-action"><a href="#term-capillary-action" class="glossary-anchor">**Capillary Action:**</a></span> The physical phenomenon where a liquid automatically creeps upward through narrow spaces or porous cellulose fibers against gravity due to surface tension and adhesive forces.
* <span id="term-cbd"><a href="#term-cbd" class="glossary-anchor">**Cannabidiol (<abbr title="Cannabidiol">CBD</abbr>):**</a></span> A non-intoxicating phytocannabinoid featuring an open resorcinol ring and a $p$-menthadienyl terpene tail; migrates at $R_f = 0.42 - 0.48$ in the Purple Canary system.
* <span id="term-cbn"><a href="#term-cbn" class="glossary-anchor">**Cannabinol (<abbr title="Cannabinol">CBN</abbr>):**</a></span> The oxidized, fully aromatized degradation product of THC featuring a planar tricyclic core; strongly retarded by cellulose paper to $R_f = 0.34 - 0.40$.
* <span id="term-chromatography"><a href="#term-chromatography" class="glossary-anchor">**Chromatography:**</a></span> The physical separation of a chemical mixture by passing it through a stationary phase (like cellulose paper) using a moving carrier solvent (the mobile phase).
* <span id="term-clia"><a href="#term-clia" class="glossary-anchor">**<abbr title="Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments">CLIA</abbr> (Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments):**</a></span> Federal regulatory standards governing diagnostic laboratory testing; forensic presumptive testing in field harm-reduction settings is CLIA-exempt.
* <span id="term-colorimetry"><a href="#term-colorimetry" class="glossary-anchor">**Colorimetry:**</a></span> The quantitative measurement and profiling of wavelength, shade, and color saturation across specific optical color channels (such as red, green, and blue RGB intensity vectors).
* <span id="term-curcumin"><a href="#term-curcumin" class="glossary-anchor">**Curcumin:**</a></span> The primary polyphenolic active compound in turmeric spice (diferuloylmethane); acts as an integrated indicator dye and natural fluorophore in the developer matrix.
* <span id="term-densitometry"><a href="#term-densitometry" class="glossary-anchor">**Densitometry:**</a></span> The analytical technique used to scan and measure light absorption, optical density, and band concentration along the vertical migration lane of a test strip.
* <span id="term-dielectric-constant"><a href="#term-dielectric-constant" class="glossary-anchor">**Dielectric Constant ($\varepsilon$):**</a></span> A fundamental measure of a solvent's polarity and ability to stabilize electrical charges; non-polar D-limonene exhibits $\varepsilon \approx 2.3$, offering negligible eluotropic power for basic amine salts.
* <span id="term-d-limonene"><a href="#term-d-limonene" class="glossary-anchor">**D-Limonene:**</a></span> A naturally occurring, food-grade terpene solvent (cyclic $p$-menthadiene hydrocarbon) cold-pressed from citrus rinds that functions as the non-polar mobile carrier.
* <span id="term-eluotropic-strength"><a href="#term-eluotropic-strength" class="glossary-anchor">**Eluotropic Strength:**</a></span> The thermodynamic capability of a solvent to desorb solutes from a polar stationary phase like cellulose and carry them upward.
* <span id="term-evali"><a href="#term-evali" class="glossary-anchor">**<abbr title="E-cigarette or Vaping Use-Associated Lung Injury">EVALI</abbr>:**</a></span> E-cigarette or Vaping Use-Associated Lung Injury; a severe pulmonary disease cluster identified in 2019 caused by the inhalation of toxic pyrolysis products from Vitamin E Acetate.
* <span id="term-false-negative"><a href="#term-false-negative" class="glossary-anchor">**False Negative:**</a></span> An analytical error where a test fails to detect a dangerous compound that is actually present; the most dangerous failure mode in harm reduction.
* <span id="term-false-positive"><a href="#term-false-positive" class="glossary-anchor">**False Positive:**</a></span> An analytical error where a test flags an adulterant or anomalous band when the sample is actually benign.
* <span id="term-fts"><a href="#term-fts" class="glossary-anchor">**Fentanyl Test Strip (<abbr title="Fentanyl Test Strip">FTS</abbr>):**</a></span> A rapid, water-based lateral flow immunoassay engineered to detect trace nanogram concentrations of fentanyl and major fentanyl analogs.
* <span id="term-fluorophore"><a href="#term-fluorophore" class="glossary-anchor">**Fluorophore:**</a></span> A chemical compound or molecule that absorbs invisible light (such as long-wave 365 nm UV-A) and re-emits it as visible, glowing luminescence.
* <span id="term-fluorescence-quenching"><a href="#term-fluorescence-quenching" class="glossary-anchor">**Fluorescence Quenching:**</a></span> The optical phenomenon where a compound absorbs ultraviolet light without glowing, appearing as a dark silhouette or shadow band against a fluorescent background.
* <span id="term-forensic-parity"><a href="#term-forensic-parity" class="glossary-anchor">**Forensic Parity:**</a></span> The principle that ordinary individuals deserve equal access to the same fundamental physical truths about what enters their bodies as state crime laboratories possess.
* <span id="term-ftir"><a href="#term-ftir" class="glossary-anchor">**<abbr title="Fourier-Transform Infrared Spectroscopy">FTIR</abbr> (Fourier-Transform Infrared Spectroscopy):**</a></span> A confirmatory optical technology that measures how molecules absorb infrared light to create an absorption spectrum.
* <span id="term-gc-ms"><a href="#term-gc-ms" class="glossary-anchor">**<abbr title="Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry">GC-MS</abbr> (Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry):**</a></span> The laboratory gold standard for confirmatory drug testing; vaporizes compounds, separates them through a capillary column, shatters them with electron beams, and weighs the fragments.
* <span id="term-good-samaritan-laws"><a href="#term-good-samaritan-laws" class="glossary-anchor">**Good Samaritan Laws:**</a></span> Statutes that provide legal immunity from drug possession arrests or prosecution for individuals who call 911 during an overdose emergency.
* <span id="term-hplc"><a href="#term-hplc" class="glossary-anchor">**<abbr title="High-Performance Liquid Chromatography">HPLC</abbr> (High-Performance Liquid Chromatography):**</a></span> A confirmatory laboratory instrument that pumps liquid solvent under high pressure through a column to separate and quantify non-volatile chemical compounds.
* <span id="term-indazole-3-carboxamides"><a href="#term-indazole-3-carboxamides" class="glossary-anchor">**Indazole-3-Carboxamides:**</a></span> A potent chemical class of synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonists (such as ADB-PINACA) lacking phenolic hydroxyl groups and migrating higher up paper strips ($R_f = 0.68 - 0.74$).
* <span id="term-keto-enol-tautomerism"><a href="#term-keto-enol-tautomerism" class="glossary-anchor">**Keto-Enol Tautomerism:**</a></span> An active chemical equilibrium where a molecule rapidly interconverts between ketone and enol structural isomers; responsible for the distinct solvent interactions and fluorophore shifts of curcumin.
* <span id="term-lod"><a href="#term-lod" class="glossary-anchor">**Limit of Detection (<abbr title="Limit of Detection">LOD</abbr>):**</a></span> The lowest physical concentration of a substance that an analytical method can reliably distinguish from background noise.
* <span id="term-matrix-masking"><a href="#term-matrix-masking" class="glossary-anchor">**Matrix Masking:**</a></span> An interference phenomenon where heavy plant waxes, dark tars, or thick unrefined oils physically trap or obscure trace adulterants on a test strip.
* <span id="term-mct"><a href="#term-mct" class="glossary-anchor">**Medium-Chain Triglycerides (<abbr title="Medium-Chain Triglycerides">MCT</abbr> Oil):**</a></span> Bulking lipids made of caprylic and capric fatty acid chains ($R_f = 0.80 - 0.88$); an illicit cutting agent in vape cartridges.
* <span id="term-mobile-phase"><a href="#term-mobile-phase" class="glossary-anchor">**Mobile Phase:**</a></span> The moving liquid solvent mixture (95.5% D-Limonene : 0.5% Curcumin w/v) that dissolves and carries chemical compounds up the chromatography strip.
* <span id="term-psa"><a href="#term-psa" class="glossary-anchor">**Molecular Polar Surface Area (<abbr title="Molecular Polar Surface Area">PSA</abbr>):**</a></span> The total surface area of all polar atoms (primarily nitrogen and oxygen) in a molecule; governs hydrogen-bonding capture on poly-hydroxyl cellulose.
* <span id="term-naloxone"><a href="#term-naloxone" class="glossary-anchor">**Naloxone (Narcan):**</a></span> An opioid receptor antagonist medication that rapidly reverses life-threatening opioid-induced respiratory depression.
* <span id="term-normal-phase-chromatography"><a href="#term-normal-phase-chromatography" class="glossary-anchor">**Normal-Phase Chromatography:**</a></span> A separation system where a polar stationary phase (cellulose paper) is paired with a non-polar or weakly polar mobile phase (terpene solvent).
* <span id="term-presumptive-testing"><a href="#term-presumptive-testing" class="glossary-anchor">**Presumptive Testing:**</a></span> Rapid point-of-care screening methods that indicate the probable presence or absence of specific compound classes without issuing quantitative molecular confirmation.
* <span id="term-rf"><a href="#term-rf" class="glossary-anchor">**Retention Factor (<abbr title="Retention Factor">$R_f$</abbr>):**</a></span> The standardized decimal fraction describing how far a specific chemical migrated relative to the total distance traveled by the solvent front ($R_f = d_{\text{analyte}} / d_{\text{solvent}}$).
* <span id="term-scra"><a href="#term-scra" class="glossary-anchor">**<abbr title="Synthetic Cannabinoid Receptor Agonist">SCRA</abbr> (Synthetic Cannabinoid Receptor Agonist):**</a></span> Potent, lab-synthesized research chemicals (such as ADB-PINACA or MDMB-4en-PINACA) engineered to bind aggressively to CB1 receptors.
* <span id="term-solvent-front"><a href="#term-solvent-front" class="glossary-anchor">**Solvent Front:**</a></span> The highest physical boundary line reached by the migrating liquid developer before the strip is pulled from the chamber ($R_f = 1.00$).
* <span id="term-sop"><a href="#term-sop" class="glossary-anchor">**<abbr title="Standard Operating Procedure">SOP</abbr> (Standard Operating Procedure):**</a></span> Standardized, sequential step-by-step instructions designed to ensure analytical repeatability and forensic precision.
* <span id="term-stationary-phase"><a href="#term-stationary-phase" class="glossary-anchor">**Stationary Phase:**</a></span> The porous solid matrix (untreated Whatman Grade 1 cellulose paper, composed of $\beta$-(1,4)-D-glucan polysaccharide fibers) that provides physical resistance during chromatography ($R_f = 0.00$).
* <span id="term-thc"><a href="#term-thc" class="glossary-anchor">**$\Delta^9$-Tetrahydrocannabinol ($\Delta^9$-<abbr title="Tetrahydrocannabinol">THC</abbr>):**</a></span> The primary psychoactive cannabinoid in natural cannabis; migrates cleanly at $R_f = 0.52 - 0.58$ in the Purple Canary system.
* <span id="term-unodc"><a href="#term-unodc" class="glossary-anchor">**<abbr title="United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime">UNODC</abbr>:**</a></span> United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime; publisher of authoritative international laboratory analytical manuals for forensic drug identification.
* <span id="term-vitamin-e-acetate"><a href="#term-vitamin-e-acetate" class="glossary-anchor">**Vitamin E Acetate ($\alpha$-Tocopheryl Acetate):**</a></span> A thick, oily synthetic ester implicated in EVALI lung injuries; migrates near the solvent front ($R_f = 0.84 - 0.92$) due to high lipophilicity.
* <span id="term-whatman-grade-1"><a href="#term-whatman-grade-1" class="glossary-anchor">**Whatman Grade 1 Cellulose:**</a></span> Pure, binder-free cotton cellulose filter paper with controlled pore size used as the standardized stationary phase.
* <span id="term-xylazine"><a href="#term-xylazine" class="glossary-anchor">**Xylazine ("Tranq"):**</a></span> A non-opioid veterinary sedative that does not respond to naloxone, increasingly co-mingled with street drug supplies.
